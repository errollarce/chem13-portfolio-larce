This project used artificial intelligence (AI) tools exclusively as an assistive aid during the development process. 
All final decisions, data verification, analysis, and creative choices were performed and validated by the creator.

Specifically, AI assistance was limited to the following tasks:

Brainstorming: Generating initial ideas and possibilities for chemical compounds.

Infographic Support: Suggesting potential taglines for visual presentation.

Editing & Refinement: Improving overall sentence flow, clarity, and wording.

Proofreading: Identifying and fixing grammatical and mechanical errors.

Research Guidance: Helping formulate and identify relevant questions to guide further investigation.

Data Initialization: Assisting with the initial setup or organization of baseline data for the compounds.

Source Clarification: Asking for clarity regarding the context and interpretation of online sources and references.
